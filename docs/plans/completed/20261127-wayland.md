# Linux desktops on Wayland: GNOME and KDE

The person's other projects ran GNOME and KDE on Wayland in UTM (nimbo's GNOME Wayland and Plasma's Wayland session, wavo-app's checks with Plasma's GlobalShortcuts portal), and trivm's Linux desktops are all X11, so trivm gets a GNOME and a KDE Plasma on Wayland to move off UTM. Done when a fresh VM of each, made by `trivm create`, logs in by itself to a Wayland session (`XDG_SESSION_TYPE=wayland` in the session), and in that session `desktop <program>` starts a native Wayland app, not one on Xwayland, and `shot`, `click x y`, `type`, `key`, `ui` and `click --name` work, each proven on the fresh VM with shots read; where `ui` or `click --name` can't work as on X11, what does and doesn't is found, written in the Result and the readme, and the rest shipped.

## Decisions

- The systems: `ubuntu-26.04-gnome`, Ubuntu 26.04 LTS's own GNOME 50, and `ubuntu-26.04-kde`, Kubuntu 26.04's KDE Plasma 6.6, both from Canonical's resolute cloud image of release-20261108 (`SHA256SUMS`), named as #13 named `ubuntu-24.04-cinnamon`. nimbo and wavo-app ran Ubuntu's GNOME and Kubuntu, at 24.04: GNOME 46 and Plasma 5.27. 24.04 has no Plasma 6, and its GNOME 46 has no GlobalShortcuts portal, which wavo-app's hotkey needs on Wayland (its readme: KDE Plasma 6, GNOME 48 and later); 26.04, the LTS after it, has GNOME 50 and Plasma 6.6, each with its portal, so one image gives a current GNOME and Plasma 6. Ubuntu's GNOME is Wayland only since 25.10. The image's qcow2 compresses with deflate (its header's compression type 0), which the reader has.
- The login: Ubuntu's GDM reads `custom.conf`, where only the automatic login is set, Wayland being its default; SDDM logs in to `plasma`, Plasma 6's Wayland session (X11's is `plasmax11`). No blanking, lock or sleep: GNOME's dconf defaults as on Debian's GNOME; Plasma's `kscreenlockerrc` and PowerDevil's AC profile in `/etc/xdg/powerdevilrc` (its keys read in `libpowerdevilcore`), not 5's `powermanagementprofilesrc`. `sddm-theme-breeze`, or apt picks Budgie's theme for SDDM.
- The network: both desktops bring NetworkManager, which netplan 1.2 won't give a NIC matched by its driver; #13's handover to it (`NM_UBUNTU`), copied as it is, so the two merge.
- `desktop` on Linux is a service of the user's systemd (`systemd-run --user`), on X11 too: the desktop gives the user's systemd its environment (`WAYLAND_DISPLAY`, `DISPLAY`, `XAUTHORITY`), so the program is a Wayland app on Wayland and an X11 one on X11, where `setsid` over SSH had only sshd's `DISPLAY=:0`, no Wayland. sshd's `DISPLAY` goes along when the desktop gave none. The unit's state is read after 0.2 s, as `setsid`'s was. systemd expands `$VAR` and `${VAR}` in a service's arguments, so each argument's `$` is doubled (`$$` is a `$` to it); the program's path, which `systemd-run` resolves itself, is left as it is. `KillMode=process`: only the program is stopped with its unit, so what it forks stays when it ends, as with `setsid`. A unit that fails within 0.2 s is read and reset; one that fails later stays listed as failed until a later call, 10 s on, resets it. A program ended by SIGHUP, SIGINT, SIGTERM or SIGPIPE is a clean end of a service to systemd, its unit collected as an exit 0's, so `desktop` reports it as one.
- agent-desktop runs with the session's `WAYLAND_DISPLAY` and `XDG_SESSION_TYPE`: over SSH alone it refuses ("the native window type … could not be proven").
- A Wayland app doesn't know where its window is: Qt gives its elements' frames in the window's coordinates, as if at 0,0; GTK 4 gives none in the snapshot, but agent-desktop keeps them, in the window's coordinates, in its refmap for each element it gives a ref (`refmap.json`); its `focus-window` refuses Wayland. So the compositor gives each window's place, stacking and focus, and raises one: on GNOME an extension of trivm's that the seed puts in (GNOME's own `org.gnome.Shell.Introspect` has no places and answers only the callers it allows), on Plasma a KWin script loaded over D-Bus, whose `console.info` is read from the journal (KWin 6's scripts have no output on D-Bus).
- agent-desktop's windows are matched to the compositor's by process, then title and size, title, size, or as the process's only one: KWin titles Plasma's desktop and panel with nothing. Twins of all three are left out of `ui`, said on stderr.
- Where an element is: a frame in the refmap, GTK 4's, from the corner of the window's frame (Mutter's frame and KWin's, the shadow out, GTK drawing its title bar in it), on Xwayland too, where agent-desktop counts it from the shadow; else the snapshot's, moved by the window's own coordinates' corner (KWin's client geometry, inside its title bar) less that of the window agent-desktop gives, so Qt's, given as if the window were at 0,0, move, and an X11 app's, already on the screen, stay.
- An open GTK 4 menu is in its window's tree with its items laid out in the window, while the compositor shows it in a popup of its own where it fits: the items are centered in that popup, the window's.

## Result

On this Mac (macOS 27.2), in a scratch XDG, fresh VMs made by `trivm create`: `ubuntu-26.04-gnome` and `ubuntu-26.04-kde`, from the resolute image, the desktops from Ubuntu's archive. The shots are `wayland-*.png` in the session's scratchpad, each read.

- GNOME: the seat's session `Type=wayland`, `Service=gdm-autologin`, gnome-shell's `XDG_SESSION_TYPE=wayland`, NetworkManager's `enp0s1` connected, the extension `ACTIVE` (`wayland-gnome-fresh.png`). `desktop ptyxis` and `desktop nautilus` have `WAYLAND_DISPLAY` and the caller's folder, with no X window of theirs on Xwayland (`wayland-gnome-desktop.png`). `type` and `key` in Ptyxis printed `typed-on-wayland: wayland` (`wayland-gnome-type.png`); `click` on Files' Documents opened it (`wayland-gnome-click.png`). `ui`'s frames, drawn over a shot, sit on Files' and Ptyxis' elements (`wayland-gnome-ui-overlay.png`). `click --name "New Terminal"` raised Ptyxis from behind Files and opened a tab (`wayland-gnome-click-name-raise.png`); `click --name "Main Menu"` opened Files' popover, its items framed in it (`wayland-gnome-menu-overlay.png`), and `"Keyboard Shortcuts"` opened its dialog (`wayland-gnome-menu-item.png`). zenity on Xwayland (`GDK_BACKEND=x11`) and on Wayland are placed alike (`wayland-gnome-zenity-overlay.png`).
- KDE: the seat's session `Type=wayland`, `Service=sddm-autologin`, `Desktop=KDE`, NetworkManager connected (`wayland-kde-fresh.png`). `desktop konsole` and `desktop dolphin`, with no X window of theirs (`wayland-kde-desktop.png`). `type` and `key` in Konsole (`wayland-kde-type.png`); `click` on Dolphin's Documents opened it (`wayland-kde-click.png`). `ui`'s frames sit on Dolphin's places, toolbar and Konsole's (`wayland-kde-ui-overlay.png`). `click --name "New Tab"` raised Konsole from behind Dolphin and opened a tab (`wayland-kde-click-name-raise.png`), `"Downloads"` raised Dolphin back and opened it (`wayland-kde-click-name.png`); under a keep-above Dolphin `click --name` refused with "is under window" (`wayland-kde-covered.png`). Dolphin's menu, open, isn't in `ui` (`wayland-kde-qt-menu.png`).
- Restarted with `stop` and `start`: each came up at its address, logged in to Wayland again, NetworkManager connected; `ui` placed Konsole (`wayland-kde-restart.png`) and Files, and `click --name Templates --double` opened the folder (`wayland-gnome-restart-click.png`).
- Never blanked or locked: GNOME after 12 minutes idle (Mutter's IdleMonitor 718923 ms), the screensaver not active (`wayland-gnome-idle.png`); KDE 12 minutes after its restart with nothing typed or clicked, the screensaver not active, no `kscreenlocker` (`wayland-kde-idle.png`).
- `desktop` on X11, now through the user's systemd: on Ubuntu 24.04's Xfce and Debian 13's GNOME the program has the desktop's `DISPLAY` and `XAUTHORITY` and the caller's folder (`wayland-x11-desktop.png`, `wayland-debian-desktop.png`); `desktop false` says "exited: 1", a missing program 127; `ui` as before. On the GNOME VM: arguments with `${HOME}`, `$VAR`, `$$`, `%h`, `a&b`, an empty one and one with a space arrive as given, and a program at `/tmp/price$tag` runs; a launcher's forked child stays after it exits; `set -e; desktop /bin/true; echo reached` reaches; five pairs of calls 0.05 s apart each report their own status; a unit that failed after 0.2 s is reset by a call 10 s on.
- macOS's `desktop` takes a program's path from zsh's `whence -p`, not `command -v`, which gave `true`, `false` and `echo` as builtins; checked in the host's zsh and the unit test, not on a macOS guest.

What doesn't work as on X11, in the readme too:

- GTK 4 gives frames only of the elements to act on (buttons, fields, cells, menu items), not of labels and groups, and some off by up to 25 pixels (zenity's buttons), their middles still on them.
- Qt's popup menus (Konsole's, Dolphin's) aren't in AT-SPI on Wayland at all: the app's tree has only its window. Their items are clicked by their place in a shot.
- A program that ends by SIGHUP, SIGINT, SIGTERM or SIGPIPE within 0.2 s is reported as started: systemd counts these as a clean end.
- GTK 3 apps on Wayland aren't checked: neither system has one.
