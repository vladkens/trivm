## Decisions

- The branch is `ui` with `older` merged in (a merge commit, no rebase); its PR's base is `ui`, so trivm#3 and trivm#6 come in with it, their conflicts resolved here.
- Conflicts: lane A's base fixes kept (`config.<pid>` for the saved config, `hold` while a VM is made, setup saved once the desktop is up, stop's 2-minute deadline and 5 s `kill`); lane B's `boot`, `ask` (VZ or QMP), `ssh::address`, `provisioned` and the Windows arms. Lane A's `NETWORK_CONFIG` matches the NIC by driver (`virtio_net`) for clones; Debian's NetworkManager takes it over with it (checked).
- On a Windows VM: `clone` and `run` work (files cloned, no machine identifier, QEMU's port the clone's own); `sip` and `grant` refuse it (macOS's); `ui` and `click --name` refuse it until agent-desktop runs there (task 6).
- trivm#1's findings: the SSH key's first use and `create`/`delete` without the lock were already fixed by lane A (`key()` under `id_ed25519.lock`; `hold` in `create`, `lock` in `delete`). Fixed here: the pid written in one padded write and read again while the lock holder writes it; the pointer mapped at the display's current size; `stop` of a guest with no address presses its power button and waits, as with SSH, and on macOS clicks Shut Down in the dialog it asks (read with Vision), and follows a starting Windows VM's pid from trivm's to QEMU's.
- `click --name`: a match inside a match is one element (GTK 4 names a cell, its twin and its label alike, as Debian's Files does); `xprop` and `xwininfo` installed with each Linux's desktop (Debian's GNOME, Fedora's Xfce and Plasma lack them).
- macOS 13 and 14: `create` marks the VM (`assistant` in its folder) when the Data volume is encrypted; `start` answers the Setup Assistant from shots read with Vision (`src/vision.rs`, shared with `sip`), each screen known by a line of its text and settled over two shots, its buttons clicked by their text, up to three tries a screen. A display asleep on the assistant left alone is woken by a key.
- Remote Login on 13 and 14: Terminal opened from Finder's Utilities (a click on the desktop, ⌘⇧U, a double click on Terminal's name, found with Vision), `sudo launchctl load -w` with the user's password, then Terminal quit and Finder's window closed once SSH answers.
  - Not from Spotlight, as first written and proven on 14: on 13 Spotlight's first use shows an introduction that takes no Return, and its list grows while it indexes, so a click on Terminal's line opened a web suggestion (two `create macos-13`s failed so).
  - Not by typing Terminal's name in Utilities and ⌘O, as proven on 13: on 14 the typed name selected nothing, after the tip that the first click on the wallpaper shows there (one `create macos-14` failed so; the double click was then tried by hand on that VM, and `start` finished its setup with it).
- Disk: the free disk fell to 17 GB during the last `create macos-14`, under the 20 GB rule. The space of the 13 VM and IPSW, deleted before, was still held by Time Machine's hourly local snapshots, which `df` doesn't count as free; the 14 VM and IPSW were deleted at once after its proof (29 GB free then). 13 wasn't made again after the double click for that: its download and install would leave about 1 GB.
- The stack-wide review's findings (pr-gate), each fixed with a test where one fits:
  - `run`'s clone is discarded through its pid file, opened before the folder's inode is checked, so another VM of the same name is never reached; its process is killed by that pid.
  - SIGKILL, from `stop` and from `run`'s discard, only to a process named as a VM's (trivm or QEMU), never to one that took a gone VM's pid since.
  - The pid file: QEMU's child writes its own pid before it execs, as it holds trivm's lock; a reader skips a gone holder's pid until the file is written again.
  - `stop` follows a starting Windows VM from trivm's process to QEMU's by its name; its requests to the VM process wait 5 s at most; the power button whenever SSH doesn't answer (not only with no address), as on 13 and 14 before Remote Login.
  - `click --name`: a point past the display's right or bottom is the display's to refuse, as it knows its size now.
  - SSH: the key made beside its place and moved in whole, private half first; `scp://` paths percent-encoded, as OpenSSH decodes them.
  - QEMU's log opened before swtpm starts, so no error between them leaves swtpm running.
  - Windows: sshd takes keys only (`PasswordAuthentication no` and `KbdInteractiveAuthentication no` put first in its config); `desktop` defined only for a script that calls it; a command line past cmd's 8191 characters (about 2,500 of script) refused with what to do instead. Not run on Windows: no ISO here (task 6).
  - A style pass: borrowed arguments, iterator chains.
- Task 6 left: Microsoft's Windows 11 arm64 ISO isn't in `~/Downloads`, and trivm takes it only by `--iso`.
- Scratch VMs in `~/.trivm-b`, its cache the shared one; each older IPSW deleted once its system was proven; 14 downloaded again to prove the Remote Login through Finder.
